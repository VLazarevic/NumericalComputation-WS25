# Numerical Computation: TU Wien (WS 2025)

This repository contains my personal work for **Numerical Computation (VU 101.973)** at **TU Wien** in the winter semester 2025/26. I use it to keep my exercise solutions, own explanations and reconstructed test questions in one place.

This is just my experience as a student, not an official course repository.

## What is in this repository?

- My solutions and notes for the problem sheets.
- Explanations that I wrote while learning the numerical methods.
- My reconstructions of **Test 1** and **Test 2**, based on what I remember from the actual tests.

## Important note about the tests

**The tests in this repository are reconstructed from my memory.** After the tests, I wrote down the tasks I still remembered. I used generative AI (GenAI) to help me formulate the questions, but the content is based on my own memory.

In my opinion, these reconstructed tests are **very, very similar to the tests I actually had**, especially the types of questions and what you had to do. Still, they are **not official exam papers**, and I cannot guarantee that every question, number or wording is exactly the same. There can also be mistakes or missing details.

Please do not treat them as official TU Wien material or as a guarantee of what will appear in a future test.

## How much time I spent

For **one problem sheet**, I usually needed around **6–8 hours** to work through the exercises and understand the solutions.

Before the **two tests together**, I studied for around **20–25 hours** in total. I repeated the exercises, went through the theory again and tried to understand the important methods, not just memorise the answers.

My estimated study time was:

| Work | Estimated time |
| --- | ---: |
| 11 problem sheets | 66–88 hours |
| Preparation for both tests | 20–25 hours |
| **Total self-study** | **86–113 hours** |

This is only an estimate of my own time. It does not include the time spent in exercise sessions or the few lectures I attended.

## How I used GenAI

For the regular exercises, I used GenAI **only for explanations and hints** when I did not understand something or got stuck. I worked on the solutions myself. This is one of the reason, why I did achieve a good grade.

## My experience with the exercise sessions

My tutor was **Lukas**, and he was a nice guy.

If you explained your solution and talked through your steps, he usually did not ask many additional questions, as long as everything was correct and understandable.
If you did not explain much or stayed quiet, he would also ask questions to check your understanding.

Overall, I had a very positive experience with him.
